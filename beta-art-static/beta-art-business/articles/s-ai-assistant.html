The problem

What usually goes wrong

General-purpose AI writes general-purpose text. It does not know your voice, your history or your standards, so every draft needs so much correction that it saves nothing. The tool is capable; the setup is missing.

The solution

What we do instead

A configured assistant with your material behind it: a voice sample, your CV, your services, your recurring formats. The prompts are written, tested and saved, so the second use is as good as the first and you are not re-explaining yourself every session.
